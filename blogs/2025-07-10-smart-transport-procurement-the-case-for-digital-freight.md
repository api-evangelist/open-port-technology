In today’s fast-moving supply chain environment, relying on manual transport procurement methods can hold businesses back. Traditional systems often mean endless phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets creating inefficiencies, higher costs, and limited transporter options. As demand grows for faster, smarter solutions, companies are rethinking how they approach transport procurement.
